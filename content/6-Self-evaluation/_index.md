During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC** from **June 22, 2026** to **August 15, 2026**, I had the opportunity to participate in building the group project **Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS**.

In this project, I took on the role of **DevOps & Documentation**, focusing on researching, deploying, and managing the AWS infrastructure using **AWS CDK**, integrating services such as **Amazon S3, AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon EventBridge, Amazon CloudWatch, and Amazon SNS**. Additionally, I participated in building the **CI/CD** pipeline with GitHub Actions and completing the project's technical documentation.

Through the internship, I not only applied the knowledge I had learned to a real-world project but also honed my ability to self-study new technologies, work in a team, manage work progress, and solve problems arising during system deployment.

## Strengths

During the internship, I always tried to complete assigned tasks on schedule and proactively learned about new technologies related to the project. Instead of just following instructions, I often read official AWS documentation, tested multiple deployment options, and found the root causes myself when encountering errors.

Besides the technical aspects, I also focused on creating documentation, updating the system architecture, and writing deployment guides to help team members easily follow and develop the project.

---

## Areas for Improvement

Although I have gained a lot of practical experience, I realize that I still need to improve some skills.

First is my communication and presentation skills within the team, so I can discuss work more confidently and effectively.

Additionally, I sometimes spend too much time optimizing or fixing a minor bug. In the near future, I need to practice time management and prioritize tasks to improve work efficiency.

At the same time, I also want to continue developing my system design and Cloud architecture mindset instead of just focusing on individual services.

---

## Development Orientation

After the internship, I plan to continue developing in the field of **Cloud Computing** and **Cloud Engineering**.

I will continue to learn more deeply about Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, the AWS Well-Architected Framework, Microservices, and system deployment solutions on the AWS platform to better prepare for future work.

## Overall Self-Assessment

The internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC** has helped me better understand the development process of a real-world Cloud system as well as how to coordinate in a group project.

For me, the greatest value is not just completing the **Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS** project, but also forming a self-learning mindset, the ability to adapt to new technologies, and a proactive spirit during work. This will be an important foundation for me to continue developing myself on the path to becoming a **Cloud Engineer** in the future.
