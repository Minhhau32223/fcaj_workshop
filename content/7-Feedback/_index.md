## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

What impressed me most throughout the program was the open and friendly learning environment. Everyone was willing to share their knowledge, provide support when I faced difficulties, and take the time to discuss possible solutions.

As an intern, I was initially a little hesitant to ask questions because I was afraid of asking something too basic. However, after some time, I realized that there were no "stupid questions" here. Every question was an opportunity to understand the problem better. This helped me become more confident when communicating and working on the project.

---

### 2. Mentor Support

The mentors closely followed our progress, explained concepts that we had not fully understood, and shared practical experience beyond what was available in the documentation.

What I appreciated most was that the mentors did not always provide the answer immediately. Instead, they often suggested a direction or recommended useful documentation for us to explore on our own.

At first, this learning approach was a little challenging because it required me to spend more time researching. However, I gradually realized that it was very effective. Finding the solution by myself helped me remember the knowledge longer and understand the underlying concepts rather than simply following instructions.

---

### 3. Relevance to My Major

The tasks I worked on were highly relevant to my Information Technology background and also aligned with my career interest in Cloud Engineering.

As part of the team project **"Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS"**, I had the opportunity to apply knowledge from university to a practical cloud-based system.

I also gained hands-on experience with several AWS services, including:

- Amazon S3
- AWS Lambda
- Amazon DynamoDB
- Amazon API Gateway
- Amazon EventBridge
- Amazon CloudWatch
- Amazon SNS
- AWS CDK

Before joining the program, I mainly knew these services at a basic theoretical level. Through the project, I gained a better understanding of how they can work together to build a complete Serverless system.

---

### 4. Knowledge and Skills Gained

After eight weeks, I gained not only AWS knowledge but also a better understanding of how to approach and develop a real-world project.

I learned how to:

- Design a system using Serverless Architecture.
- Deploy infrastructure using AWS CDK and Infrastructure as Code.
- Build APIs with Amazon API Gateway.
- Implement Event-driven Architecture using Amazon EventBridge.
- Monitor the system using Amazon CloudWatch and configure alerts with Amazon SNS.
- Build a CI/CD workflow using GitHub Actions.
- Manage source code and collaborate with team members through GitHub.
- Read technical documentation in English and independently research new technologies.

Besides technical knowledge, I also improved my teamwork, communication, task planning, and problem-solving skills.

---

### 5. Teamwork and Collaboration

One of the things I enjoyed most about the program was the spirit of teamwork.

Each member was responsible for a different part of the project, but everyone was willing to share knowledge and support each other when needed.

There were also times when the whole team spent quite a while debugging a problem. When we finally found the cause, it felt like we had just completed another feature.

These experiences helped me understand that teamwork is not simply about dividing tasks. It is also about sharing knowledge, discussing problems, and working together to achieve a common goal.

---

### 6. Overall Evaluation of the Program

For me, **First Cloud AI Journey** was more than just a program for learning AWS services. It was an opportunity to experience the development process of a cloud-based system from design and implementation to testing, deployment, monitoring, and documentation.

After eight weeks, I feel more confident when working with AWS services and designing Serverless systems. More importantly, I now have a clearer understanding of the skills and knowledge I need to continue developing my career in Cloud Engineering.

---

# Additional Questions

## What Did I Like Most?

What I appreciated most was having the opportunity to participate in building a complete project instead of learning each AWS service separately.

Throughout the project, a new component was gradually added each week, from Amazon S3 and AWS Lambda to DynamoDB, API Gateway, EventBridge, CloudWatch, and CI/CD.

Seeing the system gradually become more complete was very motivating and encouraged me to continue learning.

To be honest, there were also days when I spent a lot of time looking for a small configuration error. However, those experiences were actually some of the most memorable because they helped me understand how the system and AWS services worked in practice.

---

## What Could Be Improved?

Overall, I think the program was well organized and provided a valuable learning experience.

If there is an opportunity to improve the program in the future, I would suggest having more technical sharing sessions about real-world AWS projects. It would be valuable to hear about actual challenges that engineers face when designing and deploying systems for customers or enterprises.

It would also be interesting to organize more activities between different teams so that interns could introduce their projects, share their experiences, and even exchange stories about the bugs that took them the longest to solve.

---

## Would I Recommend the Program to Others?

**Definitely, yes.**

I would recommend this program to students who are interested in Cloud Computing and want to start learning AWS.

What I particularly liked was that the program did not only focus on how to use individual AWS services. It also helped us understand how different services can be connected to build a complete system.

I believe this practical experience is very valuable and difficult to gain through lectures, videos, or documentation alone.

---

# Suggestions and Recommendations

Some suggestions I would like to share for future programs are:

- Continue organizing technical workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions with mentors.
- Provide more mini-projects or technical challenges so interns can experience different real-world scenarios.
- Organize more activities between teams to encourage knowledge and experience sharing.
- Provide additional learning materials or video tutorials for students who are new to AWS.

---

## Conclusion

The two months I spent participating in **First Cloud AI Journey at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.** gave me valuable technical knowledge, practical experience, and lessons that would have been difficult to gain through university studies alone.

Through the team project **"Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS"**, I had the opportunity to work with AWS services, practice Serverless architecture, collaborate with teammates, and experience the process of developing and deploying a cloud-based system.

I would like to sincerely thank the mentors and the entire First Cloud AI Journey team for their guidance, support, and encouragement throughout the program.

I hope that in the future, I will have the opportunity to continue learning from AWS, contribute to more cloud projects, and perhaps one day share my own experience with future FCAJ interns.
