## General Assessment

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

The environment at **First Cloud AI Journey** is open, friendly, and highly supportive of learning. Everyone is willing to share knowledge and assist when difficulties arise. Through this program, I have become more confident in communicating and asking questions.

### 2. Support from Mentors

The mentors provided clear guidance, shared practical experiences, and often offered suggestions instead of direct solutions. This helped me proactively research and improve my ability to solve problems on my own.

### 3. Relevance to the Major

The program aligns perfectly with my **Information Technology** major and my career orientation toward **Cloud Engineering**. Through the **Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS** project, I had the opportunity to get hands-on practice with S3, Lambda, DynamoDB, API Gateway, EventBridge, CloudWatch, SNS, and AWS CDK.

### 4. Knowledge and Skills Acquired

I have improved the following skills:

- Designing Serverless Architectures.
- Deploying Infrastructure as Code with AWS CDK.
- Working with AWS services.
- Monitoring, Alerting, and CI/CD.
- Teamwork and source code management using GitHub.
- Reading technical documentation and self-studying.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The team members collaborated well and supported each other throughout the project execution. Through this, I gained a clearer understanding of the role of communication and coordination in a real-world project.

### 6. Overall Summary

**First Cloud AI Journey** gave me the opportunity to experience the entire process of building a Cloud system, from design and deployment to monitoring and CI/CD. After the program, I have a much clearer career orientation in **Cloud Engineering**.

---

## What I am Most Satisfied With

I am most satisfied with being directly involved in building the **Automatic Image Optimization System on AWS**. Implementing it myself and troubleshooting real-world errors helped me understand AWS far better than just studying the theory.

## Areas for Improvement

I hope the program can include more sharing sessions on real-world AWS architectures, mini-projects, and networking activities among the teams.

## Would I Recommend This Program to Friends?

**Yes.** I will recommend this program to friends who want to learn about **AWS and Cloud Computing**, especially those who want the chance to practice on real-world projects.

---

## Suggestions and Expectations

- Maintain the workshops and sharing sessions from mentors.
- Include more mini-projects or technical challenges.
- Enhance networking and interaction between teams.
- Provide additional documentation for AWS beginners.

---

## Conclusion

My internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC** and participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey** have helped me accumulate valuable AWS knowledge, teamwork skills, and problem-solving abilities.

This has been a memorable experience that helped me clearly define my development path in the field of **Cloud Engineering**.
