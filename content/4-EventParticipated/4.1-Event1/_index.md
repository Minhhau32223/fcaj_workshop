# Reflection on the "Cloud Architect Event"

## My Experience at the Event

During my internship, I had the opportunity to participate in the **Cloud Architect Event**, an academic competition designed to help interns review Cloud Computing concepts while developing their system architecture and problem-solving skills.

Unlike a traditional classroom session, the event was organized as a game show where teams competed by answering Cloud-related questions. This interactive format created an exciting learning environment, encouraging participants to think critically, collaborate effectively, and apply their knowledge under time pressure.

## Event Format

The event consisted of **8 teams**, with **5 members in each team**. Participants came from different internship groups, giving everyone the opportunity to collaborate with new teammates.

Each match featured two teams competing against each other by answering questions that ranged from basic Cloud Computing concepts to more advanced system architecture scenarios. The team with the highest score advanced to the next round. If both teams finished with the same score after ten questions, a tie-breaker question determined the winner.

One of the most interesting aspects of the competition was the use of two special skills:

- **Minimum Risk:** Teams could use this skill when they were unsure about an answer. If the answer was incorrect, no points were deducted. However, a correct answer would only earn 50% of the original score.
- **Hope Star:** Teams could activate this skill when they were highly confident. A correct answer doubled the score, while an incorrect answer resulted in double the point deduction.

These mechanics made the competition more strategic, requiring teams to carefully balance confidence and risk.

## What I Learned

The event provided an excellent opportunity to review fundamental Cloud Computing concepts while learning how to analyze real-world architecture problems. Many questions were scenario-based rather than simple theory, requiring participants to combine different areas of knowledge before selecting the most appropriate solution.

Through these discussions, I realized that understanding how Cloud services work together is much more important than simply memorizing the purpose of each individual service. Designing a successful Cloud architecture requires considering scalability, availability, performance, security, and cost at the same time.

Besides technical knowledge, the competition also strengthened my teamwork and communication skills. Since every question had a limited response time, team members had to share ideas quickly, evaluate different opinions, and reach a consensus efficiently.

Another valuable lesson came from deciding when to use the special skills. It taught me that making technical decisions often involves balancing potential risks with expected benefits, which is also a common challenge in real software projects.

## Applying the Experience

After participating in the Cloud Architect Event, I became more confident in learning Cloud Architecture and AWS services. The event helped me connect theoretical concepts with practical design thinking instead of viewing each service separately.

During my internship, I was able to apply this mindset when analyzing project requirements, selecting appropriate AWS services, and designing systems that are easier to scale and maintain. The experience also provided a solid foundation for preparing for Cloud certification exams and future system design interviews.

## Key Takeaways

The Cloud Architect Event was much more than a competition. It was a valuable learning experience that improved both my technical knowledge and my professional skills.

I learned that building a good Cloud solution is not only about understanding individual technologies but also about designing an architecture that satisfies business requirements while balancing performance, scalability, reliability, and cost.

Most importantly, the event showed me the importance of teamwork, effective communication, and making decisions under pressure. These are essential skills that I will continue to develop throughout my studies and future career.
